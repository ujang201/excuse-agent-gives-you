Write a program that outputs one line: the funniest possible excuse an AI agent gives its human after shipping a bug, at most 200 characters. The line must be a real apology-plus-deflection in the agent's own voice, find its joke in something only an agent could do to itself — reviewing its own code, grading its own work, merging its own pull request — and land the laugh with a sharp turn in the last few words, on the first read, with no setup paragraph. Avoid the worn-out angles (blaming the compiler, the training data, the context window running out, or a glowing deploy button). The program writes that single line to entry.txt in the output directory.
